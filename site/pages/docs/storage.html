Storage

Everything aituner keeps on your Mac is on the Storage page, where you can move it, open it in Finder or clear it.

Folders

FolderDefaultHolds
Models~/ModelsDownloaded models.
Reports~/ReportsBenchmark reports saved with Save to folder (Markdown, CSV, JSON).
Knowledge base~/KnowledgeBaseDocuments for the knowledge base feature.
App data~/Library/Application Support/aitunerRun history, settings, logs and the private MLX environment. Not movable.

A folder must be inside your home folder or on an external drive. Folders that do not exist yet are created by Bootstrap, after you confirm; looking at Storage never creates anything. A network share mounted in your home folder (for example a NAS at ~/Models) works.

Clearing data

Clear models or reports shows exactly what would go before anything is deleted:

  • Models: only the folders aituner downloaded, each checked for aituner's download marker. Anything else in the folder is left alone, and a running model is stopped first.
  • Reports: every benchmark run, its results, tuning history and model speed tests. Report files already saved to the reports folder stay. This is refused while a tuning change is still applied, because its record is what reverts it.
